## Cycle 1 (2026-11-12)

Area A — networking, crypto, transfer (reviewer: Opus 5.5 / high)

| ID | Severity | File | Summary | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A-1 | Medium | Remote/RemoteLink.swift (`remoteClosed`) | An unauthenticated `refused(notPaired)` from any phone advertising the scanner's ID deleted the remote's pairing. | Fixed: refusals never remove the pairing; shown as "Link lost (…)", the remote keeps retrying. |
| A-2 | Medium | Remote/RemoteConnection.swift, `ScannerSecrets` | Parallel `pair` handshakes passed the 2 s wrong-code pause, allowing several guesses per 2 s. | Fixed: one pairing handshake at a time (`ScannerSecrets` reserves at the hello, releases at the confirm or close); unit-tested. |
| A-3 | Medium | Remote/RemoteLink.swift (`sendScanList`) | A scan list over 1 MiB closed the link and looped through reconnects. | Fixed: `RemoteScanList.fitting` keeps the newest scans that fit one frame; unit-tested. |
| A-4 | Low | Remote/RemoteLink.swift (`fileRefused`) | After a `busy` refusal the remote asked again at once against a stale idle status. | Fixed: waits for a newer status. |
| A-5 | Low | Remote/RemoteLink.swift (`sendStatus`) | A file send stopped only at the next 4 Hz status after a recording started on the scanner's own button. | Fixed: `LineScanModel.onRecordingStart` → `scannerRecordingStarts()` stops the send at once. |
| A-6 | Low | Remote/RemoteLink.swift (`accept`) | Silent connections could hold all 4 handshake slots and lock out the paired remote. | Fixed: no hello within 3 s closes the connection; a fifth pending handshake replaces the oldest. |
| A-7 | Low | Remote/RemoteProtocol.swift (`totalBytes`), RemoteLink.swift | Peer-supplied sizes could overflow and trap. | Fixed: `RemoteFiles.isAcceptable` filters scans (plain unique names, 0–1 TB, finite times); `totalBytes` saturates; unit-tested. |
| A-8 | Low | Remote/RemoteLink.swift (`importCopy`) | The import rollback deleted a file that failed to move back. | Fixed: the folder is kept (Incomplete scans) when any file could not be moved back; the message says where. |
| A-9 | Low | Remote/RemoteLink.swift (`sendScanList`) | Two list builds could reach the remote out of order and make it delete matching staged files. | Fixed: only the newest request's list is sent. |

| F-9 | n/r | Remote/RemoteLink.swift, Remote/RemoteControlView.swift | One timeout text for every handshake stage, long network error texts, double periods, a stale pairing error on reopening. | Fixed. |
| F-10 | n/r | README.md, docs/design.md, docs/requirements.md (FR-RMT-06) | Wording did not match the code. | Fixed. |
